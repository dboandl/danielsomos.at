Psychological Counseling in Graz

When you long to be yourself again

Perhaps you are functioning in everyday life, but inside you feel numb, disconnected, or exhausted. Maybe you have experienced loss or realize: Something essential was missing in your childhood.

I accompany sensitive people who want to bring more vitality, self-contact, and inner security into their lives. My guidance is not about pressure or quick solutions, but about holding space, providing safety, and gently bringing you back into connection with yourself.

Grief counseling after loss

One focus of my work is supporting people after painful losses – after the death of a loved one, separation, miscarriage, or when a life plan falls apart. As a hospice companion (since 2018), I bring extensive experience with dying, death, and grief.

My Approach

We work at a pace that feels right for you, in a safe process that regulates your nervous system and integrates body awareness, perception, and creative approaches – e.g., painting, shaping, or imagination. This can involve past stressful experiences or formative childhood events – with appreciation and sensitivity.

  • Vitality. Consciously feeling joy, energy, and the small moments of life again.
  • Self-contact. Being back in connection with yourself, your feelings, and your needs.
  • Taking up space. Finding your own place in life, maintaining boundaries, and strengthening self-care.

By the way, creative work also happens together here: at the creative evening for groups – for 2–4 people, e.g. with friends or as a couple.

Topics where I provide effective guidance

  • Loss and grief – support through the grieving process and goodbyes
  • Emotional exhaustion, feeling overwhelmed, and self-care
  • High sensitivity – calming the nervous system and dealing with overstimulation
  • Perceiving boundaries and taking space for your own needs
  • Strengthening self-contact and inner security
  • Integrating formative experiences from childhood
  • Dealing with inner emptiness, emotional numbness, and circling thoughts
  • Life crises, questions of meaning, and reorientation
  • Finding vitality and joy in life again
  • Living abroad – homesickness, wanderlust, and arriving in a new country

Beyond that, I also provide guidance with relationship, family, and work-related topics. As an English-speaking counselor in Graz, I especially welcome expats and internationals – counseling happens entirely in English if you wish.

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I get an appointment?

You will receive a response with appointment suggestions within 24–48 hours (Mon–Fri). If it is currently urgent, please mention that – we will then see what is possible.

What are the costs?

Initial consultation: 60 minutes • 90 €
Follow-up sessions (individual): 60 minutes • 90 € or 90 minutes • 120 € – we choose together what fits better.

Do you also work online?

Yes, counseling is also available online – via video, from wherever you feel comfortable. Some people combine both: sessions in person in Graz and online. We decide together what fits better.

Do you also offer grief counseling?

Yes, grief counseling is one of my main focuses – after the death of a loved one, separation, miscarriage, or other painful losses. I also bring experience from my work as a hospice companion.

What if I'm not good at talking about feelings?

You don't have to explain anything "correctly." We start with what is currently feelable – e.g., via body awareness, small steps, and if desired, creative approaches such as painting, sculpting, or imagination.
