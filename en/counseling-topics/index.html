Counseling Topics

From loss and grief to relationships, family, work, and life crises – topics where I gently accompany you, so you can feel more vitality and self-contact again.

How I work

In my counseling, we create a safe space where you can arrive at your own pace. I draw on different approaches to support you as well as possible:

  • Mindful. At your pace.
  • Present. Anchored in the here and now.
  • Co-regulated. Holding together whatever shows up.
  • Nervous-system oriented. With the aim of regulating your nervous system a little more.
  • Body-focused.Exploring together: “What is feelable in the body right now?” – with elements from Somatic Experiencing.
  • Creative. If you wish, we use painting, shaping, or imagination as a bridge to your inner world.

Vitality & Self-Contact

Perhaps you feel empty or numb inside, or you are only functioning in everyday life. Together we create a safe space to gently come back into contact with yourself.

We explore:

  • Your inner impulses and needs
  • Your nervous system and how it can come to rest
  • Your ability to feel joy and vitality again
  • Creative ways to make the unspeakable visible

Loss & Grief

Loss can pull the ground out from under our feet. Grief is a slow process that needs a lot of space and safety.

I support you in:

  • Finding a safe place for all your feelings
  • Feeling your inner home again despite the loss
  • Activating resources that give you support

Loss and grief are a focus of my work – whether after the death of a loved one, during separation and divorce, or after a miscarriage. You can find more on the page Grief counseling in Graz & online.

Taking up space & maintaining boundaries

Often we have learned to adapt and to override our own boundaries. We work on you finding your own place in life again.

Together we strengthen:

  • Your body awareness for your boundaries
  • Your ability to stand up for yourself and your needs
  • Your inner security to take up space

Self-care & exhaustion

Self-care means taking good care of yourself – physically, emotionally, and mentally. When we neglect our needs, we often end up in a state of exhaustion.

I support you in:

  • Noticing the messages of your body
  • Perceiving your needs and taking them seriously
  • Finding the balance between giving and taking
  • Developing strategies against feeling overwhelmed
  • Cultivating healthy routines

High sensitivity & perception

High sensitivity is a gift that is often experienced as overwhelm. We work on regulating your nervous system and using your perception as a resource.

In counseling, I support you in:

  • Integrating your high sensitivity as a strength
  • Finding safety in your own body
  • Resolving inner contradictions and false beliefs
  • Regulating your nervous system in stressful phases

Partnership, being single & relationship forms

Relationships touch us where we are most vulnerable. Whether in a partnership, as a single, or in another relationship form – together we look at what wants to come alive between you and other people.

I support you with:

  • Conflicts, crises, and changes in your partnership
  • Recurring relationship patterns you would like to understand better
  • Topics around being single – chosen or unwanted
  • Questions about alternative relationship forms and how to live them in a way that feels right

Separation & divorce

A separation is often both at once: an ending and a beginning. Much starts to waver – and needs time to settle into a new order.

I support you in:

  • Grieving the loss and making sense of what you have experienced
  • Finding solid ground under your feet again
  • Realigning yourself and your life

Separation is also a form of loss – much from my grief counseling flows in here.

Family & parenting

Family is the place where closeness and friction often lie very close together. In counseling, we create space to understand family dynamics and find new ways of being with each other.

Together we address:

  • Tensions and conflicts in family life
  • Parenting questions and challenging situations with children and teenagers
  • Topics around school, learning, and exams

Life crises & decisions

Sometimes life gets out of balance – through a drastic event or a decision that feels heavy. In counseling, you find a safe setting to sort yourself out.

I support you with:

  • Personal and psychosocial crises – also as a relative or loved one
  • Taking stock: Where do I stand, what matters to me?
  • Decisions that lie ahead – carefully prepared instead of rushed
  • Reorientation after upheavals

Living abroad – homesickness & wanderlust

Living in a new country sets many things in motion: some things you leave behind, others still need to grow. I accompany expats and people living between two worlds – in English or German.

Possible topics:

  • Arriving in a new country – without losing yourself
  • Homesickness and the connection to your old home
  • Wanderlust and the longing for departure and change
  • Loneliness and building a new social network
  • Identity between cultures and languages

Work & career

Work shapes our lives – and can give us energy or drain it. Together we look at what you need to feel more clarity and energy in your working life again.

I support you with:

  • Taking stock of your career and professional reorientation
  • Burnout prevention: taking warning signs seriously before the exhaustion becomes too great
  • Dealing with bullying and distressing situations at work
  • Topics around job loss and retirement

Conflicts & supervision

Conflicts are part of life – what matters is how we meet them. For people in helping, educational, or volunteer roles, I also offer supervision.

Possible concerns:

  • Understanding conflicts and recognizing your own conflict patterns
  • Developing constructive ways of dealing with conflict
  • Supervision: reflecting on your professional or volunteer work

Your topic is not listed?

This list is not exhaustive. Every person brings their own individual topics and challenges. In an initial consultation we can look together at how I can best support you.

Note: Counseling (Lebens- und Sozialberatung) does not replace psychotherapy or medical/psychological treatment. If I notice signs of an illness, I will support you in finding appropriate help.
